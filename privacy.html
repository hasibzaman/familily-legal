Familily Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Familily ("Familily," "we," "us") is a mobile app for adults aged 18 and older that helps people in a household reflect on how things are going and, for those who choose to use it, lets partners optionally track how their relationship is doing over time. Familily is a self-reflection tool; see our Terms of Service for what it is and isn't.

This policy explains what data we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

If anything here is unclear, contact us at hasibzaman.cse@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect

1.1 Account information

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. If you sign in with Google or Apple instead of a password, we receive the name and email address your Google/Apple account provides, and an identifier that lets us recognize you on future sign-ins — we do not receive your Google/Apple password, and signing in that way does not give Familily access to your Google or Apple account beyond that.

1.2 Family membership data

Creating or joining a family in the app creates a record of: the family's name, an invite code used to add other members, and which family you belong to. Familily is for adults only (see Section 6).

1.3 Check-in entries

If you use the relationship tracker (opt-in — see Section 5), each check-in you log can include:

  • Mood (a simple low/neutral/good self-rating)
  • Whether something notable came up that day
  • Topic (a category like money, chores, parenting, etc.), if something came up
  • Resolution status (resolved / still tense / unresolved), if something came up
  • A short note, which may be text you typed yourself, or — if you used the "Tell your story" free-form entry — an AI-generated summary of what you described, which you can review and edit before saving

This is the entirety of what a check-in contains. We do not infer or collect anything about you from this data beyond what you explicitly entered.

1.4 Free-form check-in text (transient, not stored by us)

If you use "Tell your story" to describe your day in your own words instead of tapping through the structured flow, that text is sent to a third-party AI provider to turn it into the structured fields described in Section 1.3. The original free-form text you wrote is not saved in Familily's database. Only the resulting summary — which you see, can edit, and must actively confirm before it's saved — becomes part of your check-in entry. See Section 7 for detail on how that provider handles the text while processing it.

1.5 Relationship tracker consent status

Whether you've opted in or out of the relationship tracker is stored against your account. See Section 5 for how this is used and who can see it.

1.6 What we don't collect

We don't collect location data, contacts, photos, browsing history, or advertising identifiers, and we don't use any data in this app for advertising or ad tracking.

2. How we use your data

We use it to... Using...
Create and secure your account, let you sign in Account information
Connect you with the right household and show you who else is in it Family membership data
Show you your own check-in history and patterns over time Check-in entries
Turn a free-form story into structured check-in fields Free-form check-in text (transiently)
Decide whether to show you a shared/joint insights view Relationship tracker consent status (yours and, if mutual, your partner's)

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

3. Who we share data with

3.1 Within your family

  • Your name and the fact that you're a member of the family are visible to other members of the same family — that's how a family app works.
  • Your check-in entries are visible to nobody but you, unless you and a specific partner have both independently opted in to the relationship tracker — see Section 5. They are never visible to a family member who hasn't opted in, and never visible to anyone outside your family, under any circumstances.
  • Whether you personally have opted in or out of the relationship tracker is not shown to anyone else unless the other person has also opted in — a one-sided opt-in reveals nothing to the other side.

3.2 Service providers (processors)

We use a small number of third-party services to run the app. They process data on our behalf, under contract, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes:

Provider What they process Why
Supabase All account, family, and check-in data described in Section 1 Database, authentication, and backend hosting for the app
Anthropic Free-form check-in text (transiently; see Section 7) Parsing a free-form story into structured check-in fields
Google / Apple Name and email address, only if you choose to sign in with Google or Apple Lets you sign in without creating a separate password

3.3 What we never do

We never share your data across families, never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never share it with any third party beyond the processors listed above, except where required by law (for example, a valid legal order).

4. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active.

Deleting your account: Familily has a self-serve "Delete my account" option in Settings. Confirming it immediately and permanently deletes your check-ins, your relationship-tracker consent status, your account record, and your sign-in credentials — this happens right away, not after a waiting period. If you're the last adult in your family (or its only member), your family's data is deleted too; if other family members remain, only your own data is removed and everyone else is unaffected. If you'd rather not use the in-app option, you can also request deletion by emailing hasibzaman.cse@gmail.com.

We retain data only as described above, except where we're required to keep something longer for legal, security, or dispute-resolution reasons.

5. The relationship tracker is self-reported, and independently opted into

The relationship tracker is entirely optional and entirely self-reported — nothing is measured automatically, and nothing is inferred about you beyond what you type or select.

  • Opting in is a personal, independent choice. Each adult in a family decides for themselves whether to opt in. One partner opting in does not opt the other partner in, and does not give either partner access to the other's data.
  • Shared insights require full mutual consent. A joint/shared view combining two partners' check-in patterns only appears once both partners have opted in. If only one has opted in — or neither has — nothing is shared, and each person's check-ins remain visible only to them.
  • This is re-checked live, not assumed. If a partner revokes their consent, shared insights disappear immediately — on the very next time either partner opens that view, not after some delay or a separate sync step.
  • Sharing never crosses families, and never involves anyone outside the two consenting adults within one family. A family member who hasn't opted in never sees shared insights, and is never identified to the opted-in pair as "someone who hasn't opted in" — they simply don't appear.

6. Age requirement; no data from children

Familily is for adults aged 18 or older. You must confirm that you are 18 or older to create an account, and the app has no feature for adding or creating accounts for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete that account and its data.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at hasibzaman.cse@gmail.com and we will delete it.

7. AI processing of free-form check-in text

When you use "Tell your story," the text you write is sent to Anthropic (the maker of the Claude AI models), through Anthropic's commercial API, to be parsed into the structured mood/topic/resolution/summary fields you then review. Anthropic acts as our service provider for this request, not as an independent owner of your data.

What Anthropic's current API documentation says about that data (their policy, which can change — see Anthropic's API and data retention page):

  • Not kept by default. Anthropic states that prompts and responses sent through its API are not retained by default beyond what is technically necessary to return the response. We have not arranged any special data-retention agreement with Anthropic, so we rely on these default terms.
  • Not used to train models. Anthropic states that retained data is never used for model training without the customer's express permission, and we have not given it.
  • Exceptions. Anthropic may retain inputs and outputs where the law requires it, or where its automated trust-and-safety systems flag the content — in which case it may keep them for up to 2 years. Some newer Anthropic models carry a mandatory 30-day retention requirement; if we ever move to one of those, we will update this policy first.
  • Our database never stores your original text. Only the AI-generated summary, after you've had a chance to review and edit it, is saved as part of your check-in entry (Section 1.4).

Because the text is sent over the internet to a third party, please don't include information you wouldn't want processed this way (for example other people's contact details or financial account numbers). You can always use Quick Check instead, which doesn't send any free text to Anthropic.

8. Security

Access to your data is controlled by database-level access rules, not just app-level checks: your check-in entries are only ever readable by you, or by a specific partner who has independently and mutually opted in with you, enforced at the database layer. Data is encrypted in transit. We follow the principle of collecting only what the app needs to function (Section 1.6).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these, contact us at hasibzaman.cse@gmail.com.

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where required, notify you in the app.
